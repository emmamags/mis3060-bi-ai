# Business Context Exploration
## Question 1:
### 1. Exact Prompt  
"The portfolio provides 3 borrower attributes (credit score, debt-to-income ratio, annual income). What makes a credit score an important and unique attribute to include in comparison to the other three? How highly should a portfolio manager regard the stat?"
### 2. Summary
Claude states that credit score is very important to include as a borrowing attribute because it tracks a borrower's repayment history over time. DTI and annual income, on the other hand, reflect only current financial ability and don't account for the borrower's habits and past behavior. It also states that "credit score is the clearest driver of risk differentiation," which means that, for a portfolio manager, credit score should rank highest among the three stats.
